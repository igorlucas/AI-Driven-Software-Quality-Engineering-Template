# Maintainability review guidance

- Check clarity, cohesion, duplication, naming, complexity, and consistency with established patterns.
- Ensure abstractions reduce real complexity and errors are explicit and diagnosable.
- Evaluate test isolation, setup, assertions, and future change cost.
- Review documentation and operational instructions affected by behavior changes.
- Avoid style-only blocking comments where formatting automation can decide.

AI suggestions should identify a concrete maintenance consequence and a low-cost remedy rather than request refactoring by preference.
