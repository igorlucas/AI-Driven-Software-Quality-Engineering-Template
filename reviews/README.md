# Engineering reviews

Review changes while they are being developed, not as a substitute for tests or accountable approval. Choose review lenses based on changed behavior and risk; link findings to task, risk, criterion, and evidence IDs as appropriate.

AI may identify candidate issues, but reviewers must verify each finding against the actual diff and context. Reject speculative findings, request a concrete correction for actionable issues, and do not paste secrets or private customer data into unapproved AI services.
