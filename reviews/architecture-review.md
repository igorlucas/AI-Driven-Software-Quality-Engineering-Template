# Architecture review guidance

- Check fit to system boundaries, ownership, and documented architecture decisions.
- Evaluate data flow, coupling, failure isolation, dependency direction, and migration/rollback.
- Review scalability, availability, consistency, and operational complexity against stated requirements.
- Identify security/privacy boundaries and resilience implications.
- Record material trade-offs and decision owners; add `DEC` records when a durable decision is made.

Review only architecture implications supported by the change and context; phrase unresolved assumptions as questions.
