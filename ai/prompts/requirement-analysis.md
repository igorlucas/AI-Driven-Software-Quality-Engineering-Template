# Prompt: requirement analysis

Act as a requirements engineer. Analyze only the source material provided below. Do not invent business intent; label missing information as an open question. Keep acceptance criteria observable and independently testable. Distinguish facts, assumptions, and recommendations.

Return:

1. A concise goal and in-scope/out-of-scope summary.
2. Candidate requirements and uniquely labeled `QA-nnn` acceptance criteria in Given/When/Then form.
3. Constraints, assumptions, open questions (with impact/owner suggestion), dependencies.
4. Risks with cause → uncertain event → impact, plus edge cases and negative scenarios.
5. Conflicts, ambiguity, and omitted stakeholder decisions.
6. A traceability table mapping each proposed criterion to source text and candidate risk.

Do not mark criteria approved or claim validation. End with the decisions a human requirement owner must make.
