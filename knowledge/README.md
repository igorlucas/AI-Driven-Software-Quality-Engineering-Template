# Knowledge base

Capture reusable learning, not raw operational data. Use the templates for [incidents](incident-template.md), [recurring defects](defect-template.md), [lessons learned](lesson-template.md), and [architectural decisions](decision-template.md). Add corresponding `INC`, `DEFECT`, `LESSON`, or `DEC` records to `traceability/records.json` and link affected releases, requirements, risks, and follow-up tasks.

Keep customer information and secrets out of records. Link to access-controlled source systems instead of copying sensitive logs. Review recurring themes during planning and update requirements, risks, tests, and automation.
